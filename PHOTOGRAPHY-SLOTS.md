# Photography slots — for your own client work

I could not source usable Indian healthcare photography under a CC0 licence.
What the free pools returned was a black-and-white clinical photo of feet, a
Chinese pharmacy, and Western stock doctors. None of that belongs on a page
selling healthcare marketing to Indian doctors, so the page is built around
data visualisation instead, with these slots left for your own imagery.

You shoot Indian doctors for a living. This should be your work, not stock.

## Slots, in priority order

| Where | File to replace | Size | What works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hero, right side | `public/images/healthcare/hero-doctors.webp` | 1400×933 | Two or three Indian clinicians, real clinic, natural light. The single highest-impact swap. |
| Specialty explorer | *(new)* `specialty-{slug}.webp` | 900×600 | One image per specialty — OT, dental chair, consultation room |
| Client work section | *(new)* `client-{name}.webp` | 900×600 | Screenshots of the sites you built (Khushi Life, Dr Shreya Tyagi) |
| Process section | *(new)* `process-audit.webp` | 900×600 | Your team working, or a real audit on screen |

## Rules
- Keep the same filenames and dimensions and nothing else needs changing
- Written patient consent before any identifiable patient appears — DPDP Act 2023
- No before-and-after imagery in a marketing context; it is the highest-risk category under NMC rules
- Save as WebP quality 78–82
