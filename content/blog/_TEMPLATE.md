Open with the answer. Not a preamble, not "in today's landscape" — the actual
answer to the question in the title, in about 40 to 60 words. This paragraph is
what an AI assistant lifts when it cites you, and what a reader uses to decide
whether to keep going.

## Use ## for every section heading

Regular paragraphs are just text with a blank line between them. Write short
ones. Two to four sentences.

You can **bold something** that matters, *italicise* a term you are introducing,
and use `inline code` for anything technical.

Link out to [a source with a real name](https://example.com) — external links
open in a new tab automatically. Link inward to
[our performance marketing work](/services/performance-marketing) — internal
links navigate without reloading the page.

### Sub-headings use three hashes

Bulleted lists:

- Keep items to one line where you can
- Parallel structure — start each one the same way
- Three to six items. More than that is a table

Numbered lists work when order matters:

1. First thing
2. Second thing
3. Third thing

> A blockquote becomes a highlighted callout. Use it once per article, for the
> single sentence you would want someone to remember.

Tables work for anything comparative:

| What | Then | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Refresh window | 45–60 days | 21–35 days |
| Assets needed | 4 a month | 15–30 a month |

You can drop an image into the body. The text in brackets is what a screen
reader announces; the quoted part is the caption a reader sees. Leave the
quoted part off and there is no caption.

![Describe the image here](/images/blog/some-diagram.webp "Caption under the image")

Two or three images side by side, for before-and-after:

:::images
![The original creative](/images/blog/before.webp "Before")
![The rebuilt version](/images/blog/after.webp "After")
:::

A single number you want people to remember:

:::stat
21–35 days
is how long a creative now lasts before it fatigues. It used to be 45–60.
:::

A YouTube or Vimeo link on its own line becomes a player:

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xxxxxxxxxxx

And a call to action, which is a sentence and a button:

:::cta
This is the loop we run for D2C brands every month.
/services/performance-marketing | See how performance marketing works
:::
