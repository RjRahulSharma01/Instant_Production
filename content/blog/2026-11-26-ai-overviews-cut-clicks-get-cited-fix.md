If your blog still ranks but enquiries from it have thinned out, the ranking is not the problem. Ahrefs found that when an AI Overview sits above a query, the page in position one gets 58% fewer clicks. The fix is to write pages the AI answer quotes and links, because cited brands keep noticeably more of the traffic that is left.

## What the numbers say

Two studies are worth reading in full, because they measure different things.

Ahrefs compared Google Search Console data for 300,000 keywords, half with an AI Overview and half informational keywords without one, across December 2023 and December 2025. For the top result on AI Overview keywords, average click-through rate fell from 0.073 to 0.016. On comparable keywords with no AI Overview it fell from 0.076 to 0.039. Ahrefs puts the gap at a 58% lower click-through rate, up from the 34.5% it measured in April 2025.

Seer Interactive looked at 3,119 informational search terms across 42 client organisations from June 2024 to September 2025, and split the results by whether the brand was cited inside the AI Overview.

| Q3 2025, informational queries | Organic CTR | Paid CTR |
|---|---|---|
| AI Overview present, brand not cited | 0.52% | 4.14% |
| AI Overview present, brand cited | 0.70% | 7.89% |
| No AI Overview | 1.45% | 13.88% |

Seer's summary: cited brands got 35% more organic clicks and 91% more paid clicks than brands that were not cited. Seer is careful to say it cannot prove citation causes the lift. Even so, the direction is the same in both studies, and it is the one fact a content plan can act on.

## Why this matters more in India

Google brought AI Mode to India as a Labs experiment in English on 24 June 2025, and said that in the US and India, AI Overviews were already driving a more than 10% increase in usage for the queries where they appear. By October 2025 AI Mode had added Hindi plus Bengali, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu and Urdu, and Search Live launched in India in English and Hindi. Google also calls India its largest user base for voice and visual search.

So the questions your buyers type or speak on a phone, in Hindi or English, are exactly the ones most likely to be answered before anyone scrolls to your link. The buyer still forms a shortlist. The 6sense 2025 Buyer Experience Report found 94% of buying groups rank preferred vendors before first contact. The question is whether your name is in the answer they read while doing it.

## Diagnose it in 30 minutes

Before rewriting anything, confirm the pattern is real for you.

### Check 1: impressions up, clicks flat

In Search Console, open the Performance report for the last 16 months and compare your top 20 blog pages. Rising or steady impressions with falling clicks and a stable average position is the signature of an answer box taking the click. A falling position is a different problem: ordinary ranking loss.

### Check 2: search your own queries

Take the five queries that used to send you the most enquiries. Run each on Google on a phone, in English and once in Hindi, and note whether an AI Overview appears and who it cites. Ask the same question in ChatGPT and Gemini. Write down every brand named. If competitors, aggregators or a Reddit thread are cited and you are not, you have found the gap.

### Check 3: read your own page as a machine would

Open the page and look at the first 100 words. If they are an introduction about "today's fast-paced world" rather than the answer, there is nothing in them worth quoting. That opening is the part an answer engine is most likely to lift, so it has to carry the answer.

## The fix you can apply yourself

None of this needs a special AI tactic. AI answers draw on pages that are indexed, clear and specific. These are the changes that make a page quotable.

1. **Answer first.** Put a direct, two or three sentence answer to the page's main question at the very top, with the number, the rule or the recommendation in it. The rest of the page is the evidence.
2. **One question per section.** Use subheads written as the questions buyers actually ask, followed by the answer in the first sentence under each. A section that answers one thing cleanly can be lifted on its own.
3. **Name real things.** The regulation, the report, the date, the figure, the city. "Studies show" gives a summariser nothing to attribute. "Seer Interactive, 3,119 queries, Q3 2025" does.
4. **Add something only you have.** Your own pricing ranges, turnaround times, process steps, a comparison table you built, a checklist from your own projects. If your page says what twenty others say, the answer can cite any of them. Original detail is the reason to cite you.
5. **Make the brand legible.** State plainly who you are, where you work and what you do on the page and the About page, consistently with your Google Business Profile and LinkedIn. Answers name brands they can identify without guessing.
6. **Keep it fresh and dated.** Show a visible updated date and revise numbers when they change. A 2023 statistic on a 2026 question is easy to pass over.
7. **Fix the basics.** Pages blocked from crawling, buried behind scripts or slow to load on a mid-range phone cannot be read, let alone cited. Check your important pages are indexed in Search Console.

Then change what you measure. Track, monthly, which of your 20 priority questions show an AI Overview, whether you are cited, and what enquiries came from organic search. Clicks per page alone will keep looking worse even when the content is working.

> Ranking is now the entry ticket. The citation is what gets read.
