Our monthly report opens with four numbers: qualified enquiries, pipeline value, blended customer acquisition cost (CAC) and revenue we can trace to the work. Platform ROAS sits further down, labelled as what it is: the ad platform's own estimate. We will not put a guaranteed ROAS in a proposal, and this post explains why that is a feature of how we work rather than a hedge.

## The problem with leading on ROAS

ROAS as Meta or Google reports it is the platform measuring the platform. It counts conversions it can see, inside attribution windows it sets, using modelled data where tracking breaks. That number is useful for steering bids. It is a poor answer to the question a founder actually asks, which is whether the money came back.

The windows themselves keep moving. From 12 January 2026, Meta stopped returning the 7-day view and 28-day view attribution windows in its Ads Insights API; the 1-day click, 7-day click, 28-day click, 1-day engaged-view and 1-day view windows remain. Meta said the change would reduce discrepancies with Ads Manager. Any dashboard that had been quietly reporting 28-day view conversions saw its "results" shrink overnight with no change in the business.

> If a report can swing by a third because a platform changed a setting, it was never measuring your business.

The other issue is cost pressure. The ET BrandEquity x Ipsos State of Digital Marketing in India 2025 report names ad fatigue and rising CAC among the critical hurdles Indian marketers face. When acquisition cost is climbing, an in-platform ROAS that looks steady usually means the platform is crediting itself with sales that would have happened anyway, from repeat buyers, branded search or word of mouth.

## Why clients leave agencies

The Setup 2025 Marketing Relationship Survey, run with the CMO Institute on roughly 100 responses, found that 61% of clients who ended an agency relationship did so over delivery and 61% over value. The value figure was 36% a year earlier. Another 44% left because the agency did not understand their business.

Agencies in the same survey believed the top reason clients leave was budget cuts. That gap is mostly a reporting problem. An agency that reports impressions, reach and platform ROAS can look busy and successful for months while the client's finance team sees no movement. By the time the two views collide, the relationship is already over.

## What our report actually shows

Here is the structure we use, and the question each line answers.

| Line in the report | Where the number comes from | Question it answers |
|---|---|---|
| Qualified enquiries | Your CRM or lead sheet, after your team's qualification | Did real buyers raise their hand? |
| Pipeline value | Enquiries multiplied by your own deal values and stage | What could this turn into? |
| Blended CAC | Total marketing spend divided by new customers | What is a customer costing all-in? |
| Attributed revenue | Your sales or order data, matched to source | What came back? |
| Platform ROAS | Meta and Google, with the window stated | How is the bidding algorithm doing? |
| Creative health | Hook rates, frequency, days live per asset | What needs replacing next? |

The first four lines come from your systems, not ours or the platforms'. That is deliberate. If a number cannot be checked against something your finance or sales team already trusts, it does not lead the report.

### Enquiries, not leads

A lead is a form fill. An enquiry, in our reports, is a lead your team has looked at and agreed is worth a call. For a clinic that might mean a patient asking about a specific treatment; for a real estate developer, a buyer with a budget inside the project's range; for a coaching institute, a parent of a student in the right class. We agree that definition with you in the first week and do not change it to make a month look better.

### Blended CAC beside platform cost per result

Platform cost per result only counts what the platform could attribute. Blended CAC divides all marketing spend, including production and our fee, by all new customers. The two together tell you something neither does alone: if platform cost per result is falling while blended CAC rises, spend is moving towards people who were going to buy anyway.

### Creative health, because creative is the lever

In competitive Indian categories a creative typically fatigues in 21 to 35 days, and three or four active ad sets usually need 15 to 30 assets a month. So the report tracks how long each asset has been live, its frequency and its hook rate, and lists what we are replacing next month. That is where most of the performance actually comes from, and it is where our production side and media side work off the same page.

## Why we will not guarantee a ROAS

We get asked for a ROAS guarantee in most pitches. We decline every time, for three reasons.

First, the number is not ours to control. Your pricing, stock, website speed, sales follow-up and seasonality all move it. An agency that guarantees it is either going to manage to the platform's flattering windows or quietly move budget into branded search and retargeting, where ROAS looks high because the buyers were already yours.

Second, it pushes the wrong behaviour. Managing to a ROAS target usually means cutting prospecting, which starves next quarter's pipeline to make this month's dashboard look good.

Third, it is the clearest sign of a poor fit. We say plainly that we are not the right agency for brands that want guaranteed ROAS, for ad accounts under about ₹2 lakh a month, or for anyone whose headline outcome is follower count. Saying that early saves both sides a few months.

What we commit to instead is the process: a continuous testing loop, a hook bank you keep, a creative refresh cadence matched to fatigue, and a report built on your numbers that tells you plainly when something is not working.

## How this works with an in-house team

Many of the brands we work with already have someone running the ad account. A common setup is that we run creative volume and testing, and the in-house team keeps ownership of the account and the budget. The report still leads with enquiries and CAC, which means both teams are judged on the same outcome rather than arguing over whose attribution is right.

You also own everything we make: masters, versioned exports and prompt documentation. If you leave, your data, your hook bank and your account stay with you.

## What to ask any agency, including us

Before you sign, ask to see a sample monthly report with client details removed. Check three things. Does it start with numbers from your systems or theirs? Does it state which attribution window the platform figures use? Does it say what will change next month, or only what happened last month?
